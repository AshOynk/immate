# Political Expenses Tracker

A self-contained website that explores MPs' business costs & expenses **by party**,
**by individual**, and **compares average claims across parties**.

No build step, no server, no dependencies — it's plain HTML/CSS/JS. Open
`index.html` or serve the folder statically.

## Features

- **Overview** — headline totals, average claim per MP by party, total spend by
  party, and where the money goes by category.
- **By Party** — a card per party (average per MP, total, deviation from the
  national average). Click a party for its category breakdown and member list.
- **By Individual** — a searchable, sortable, filterable table of every member,
  each shown against their **party average** and the **national average**. Click a
  member for a full breakdown and a comparison chart.
- **Compare** — pick any two parties for a head-to-head on average claim and a
  per-category comparison, plus a diverging chart of every party vs the national
  average.
- **Light / dark theme**, hover tooltips on every chart, keyboard-free navigation.

## Data

The site ships with an **illustrative, synthetic dataset** (`assets/data.js`,
generated by `build-data.mjs`). The figures are *not real* — they are modelled on
the structure and typical ranges of the UK's official
[IPSA](https://www.theipsa.org.uk/mp-staffing-business-costs) "MPs' staffing and
business costs" publications (staffing dominates; London-area members claim little
accommodation; travel scales with distance from Westminster). Party sizes mirror
the 2024 General Election result so the by-party averages behave realistically.

### Loading real figures

Click **Import CSV** (top right) — or drag a `.csv` onto the page — to replace the
demo data with a real dataset. The importer auto-detects columns by header name:

| Field | Header names it recognises |
|---|---|
| MP name *(required)* | `MP Name`, `Name`, `Member`, `MP` |
| Party *(required)* | `Party` |
| Constituency | `Constituency`, `Seat` |
| Region | `Region`, `Area` |
| Staffing | `Staffing`, `Staff` |
| Office | `Office Costs`, `Office` |
| Accommodation | `Accommodation`, `Housing` |
| Travel | `Travel`, `Travel & Subsistence` |
| Other | `Other`, `Winding`, `Start-up` |
| Total | `Total`, `Amount` (used if no category columns) |

Amounts may include `£` and thousands separators. If a member appears on multiple
rows, their category amounts are summed. Download the official annual "individual
business costs" CSV from IPSA and import it directly.

**Export** downloads the current (filtered) table as CSV.

## Regenerating the demo data

```bash
node build-data.mjs   # rewrites assets/data.js
```

## Why synthetic by default?

The live IPSA data hosts were unreachable from the build environment, and attaching
unverified figures to real, named individuals would be misinformation. The demo
data is clearly labelled as synthetic throughout the UI, and the CSV importer is the
first-class path to real, attributable figures.
